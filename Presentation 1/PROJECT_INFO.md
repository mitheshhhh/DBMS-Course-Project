## PROBLEM STATEMENT

### Time-Consuming Operations
Manual lot management introduces delays and human error at every stage of the parking workflow.

### Manual Billing & Reservations
Tracking reservations and calculating charges by hand leads to billing errors and revenue loss.

### No Real-Time Visibility
Staff and drivers have no live insight into which spaces are available, causing congestion and frustration.

### No Analytics Capability
Without structured data, it is impossible to analyze usage patterns or make informed operational decisions.

---

## OBJECTIVE

- Develop a centralized database system for efficient smart parking lot management.

- Manage parking facilities, floors, zones, slots, vehicles, customers, and vehicle types.

- Manage parking passes, bills, payments, and reservation expiry.

- Record vehicle entry and exit accurately to prevent slot conflicts and unauthorized usage.

- Automate parking fee calculation based on parking duration and applicable tariffs.

- Enable real-time slot search, allocation, reservation, and availability tracking.
